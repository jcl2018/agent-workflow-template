# Plan: prep-budget

Goal file: .loop/goals/prep-budget.md (done lines frozen). Design: .loop/goals/prep-budget.design.md (read it first: its Premises
1-6 are the rules this plan implements). D below is skills/workflow-delivery/scripts.

## Implementation plan

### Context

A goal lane's rows stop at test_budget_minutes (default 15, floor 15) and the whole lane is killed at the flow file's limit or
test_budget_minutes plus five minutes, but the work before the first row is bounded only by 4x that limit: lane.py's lane()
runs lanekit.prep (re-render and commit the Tests tables, up to 300 s render plus 60 s commit), then starts a child
(`lane.py lane-exec`) that runs regression.py: planning (tiers.plan), then slots.acquire (a .queued marker while it waits for
a slot) and slots.started() (the .started marker, written when the rows begin). lane.py is 20478 bytes, two bytes under the
20480-byte script cap, so new logic goes in lanekit.py (16.7 KB) after small helpers move there. Scripts that must stay
identical across skills (lanekit.COPIED, copies of slots.py) are not touched.

### Changes

1. D/budget.py: DEFAULTS gains prep_budget_minutes 15 (the floor and the refusal text are the existing ones); a `prep_seconds(cfg)`
   returns the setting in seconds, or the test-only LOOP_PREP_BUDGET (whole seconds, never read from config); `lines()`
   and a new `label(hit)` name a kind "prep" hit "preparation".
2. D/lanekit.py: `prep_note(held, times, cut)` builds the "before rows: prep Xs, planning Ys, slot wait Zs" text and, for a
   cut, the LANE_PREP_BUDGET text naming the part that ran long; `phase_times(marks)` computes planning and wait from the
   lane child's marks; helpers `read_json`, `set_last`, `mtime`, `drop` move here from lane.py (lane.py imports them) so lane.py
   ends under the cap.
3. D/lane.py: lane() times its prep call and refuses with LANE_PREP_BUDGET before starting when prep alone reached the budget;
   otherwise the job carries prep_s and prep_by (the budget's end, counted from before prep), and the child gets it as
   _LOOP_LANE_PREP_BY; deadline() answers prep_by while no .started marker exists (the old 4x rule stays for a job with no
   prep_by); lane_exec() records the first-seen times of the .queued and .started markers, kills its group at prep_by before
   the rows begin (exit 125, a .prepcut file naming planning or waiting for a slot) and writes a .times file; finalize() adds
   the before-rows timings to every LANE line and to the .last record (prep_s, plan_s, wait_s), and on a prep cut returns the
   LANE_PREP_BUDGET line, records the hit (budget.hit kind prep, with the part and the three seconds) on the goal's run
   file and fails the lane so the goal runs the same command again; stop() also removes .prepcut and .times.
4. skills/workflow-monitoring/scripts/monitor.py and skills/workflow-health/scripts/lean.py: the budget-hit lines name a prep
   hit "preparation" and its part (inline, since a skill imports only its own scripts).
5. Setting wiring: skills/workflow-setup/scripts/settings.py registry entry, references/settings.md, skills/loop-files.md,
   skills/workflow-docs/references/start.md, skills/workflow-regression/references/commands.md (a LANE_PREP_BUDGET row in the
   lane messages table, the lane section, LOOP_PREP_BUDGET as test-only), .loop/config.json prep_budget_minutes 15, and
   .loop/START.md regenerated by the entry command.
6. docs/wiki/Regression.md REG-7 gains the owner's sentence (Unreviewed) and docs/wiki/Delivery.md DEL-8 a sentence for the record and
   the board; both pages are Unreviewed; Tests tables re-rendered; CHANGELOG [Unreleased] entry.
7. dev/toy/test-prep-budget.sh (new, no model): the setting defaults to 15 and a value under 15 is refused with the fix line;
   a lane that waits for a slot past a tiny LOOP_PREP_BUDGET stops with LANE_PREP_BUDGET naming "waiting for a slot", ran no
   row and committed nothing; a lane's .last and LANE line carry the three durations; the same command run again after the slot
   frees passes; the goal's run file's budget_hits holds the prep hit and the monitoring report and health list it with the
   part; a lane within the budget is unchanged. .loop/regression.tsv rows reg7-prep-floor, reg7-prep-stop, reg7-prep-times,
   reg7-prep-rerun, del8-prep-board, del8-prep-health, each seen failing under a planted break then passing.

### Tests

and test-lanes.sh pass unchanged; the size-budget row stays green (every script under 20480 bytes); the full land lane passes and

exists); the flow file's limit and test_budget_minutes behaviour; reviewing REG-7 or DEL-8 (the owner does, at the PR).
